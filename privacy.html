Privacy Policy for rclone-personal

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Purpose

rclone-personal is a private, single-user application used by its owner to synchronize files between the owner's computer and the owner's Google Drive account using rclone.

Google data accessed

The application may access Google Drive files, folders, and related metadata solely to perform synchronization operations requested by the owner.

Use and storage of data

Google Drive data is processed locally by rclone on the owner's computer. This application does not operate an independent server and does not collect Google Drive data into an external database. Local synchronized copies remain on the owner's computer.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used for any purpose unrelated to the owner's personal file synchronization.

Authentication

OAuth authorization tokens are stored locally in the owner's rclone configuration. The application does not collect or store the owner's Google password.

Retention and deletion

Files remain in Google Drive and on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them. The owner can revoke the application's Google account access at any time and can remove the local rclone configuration and synchronized files.

Contact

Questions may be directed to the owner of the GitHub repository hosting this policy.
